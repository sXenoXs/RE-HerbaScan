How the App Works — Offline vs. Cloud-Sync Mode

HerbaScan operates in two distinct modes. The mode you use determines what data, if any, is processed and stored.

Default · Primary

Offline Mode

  • Photos processed entirely on your device
  • Uses an embedded TFLite MobileNetV2 AI model
  • No image ever leaves your phone
  • Results saved to local SQLite database only
  • No network connection required

Zero personal data collected.

Optional · User-Initiated

Cloud-Sync Mode

  • Requires creating a HerbaScan account
  • Email + hashed password stored on Supabase
  • Only scans you explicitly choose to save are uploaded
  • Auto-save is OFF by default
  • Delete your cloud data at any time

Only activates when you opt in.

Camera and Device Permissions

4.1 Camera Permission

Requested solely to allow you to photograph plant specimens for AI-powered identification. The camera activates only when you explicitly initiate a scan.

Image Processing Declaration: All images captured via the camera are processed locally on-device using an on-device TensorFlow Lite model. No image data is transmitted to HerbaScan servers or any third party as part of the core identification workflow. This applies at all times in Offline Mode.

We do not take photos without your knowledge, record video, transmit photographs externally (in Offline Mode), or access any image not explicitly selected by you for a scan.

4.2 Photo Library / Gallery Permission

Requested only if you choose to select an existing image from your device gallery. The same local-processing guarantee applies.

4.3 Storage Permission

Requested on certain Android versions to enable saving scan history to local SQLite storage and exporting scan cards to your device.

4.4 Internet / Network Permission

Requested to enable optional cloud sync (Personal Herbarium), synchronization of the plant catalog on launch, and admin-only features. The core plant identification feature does not require or use an internet connection.

4.5 Notification Permission

Requested solely to enable preparation-step timer notifications when using the Preparation Guide feature. Notifications are local and generated on-device. No notification content is transmitted to any server.

Legal Basis for Processing (Philippine DPA — RA 10173)

All processing is conducted in strict compliance with Republic Act No. 10173 and its Implementing Rules and Regulations.

7.1 Consent (Section 12(a), RA 10173)

When you create an account, enable cloud sync, or submit user feedback, you provide free, informed, specific, and unambiguous consent to our processing of your data.

7.2 Contractual Necessity (Section 12(b), RA 10173)

Processing your account credentials is necessary to provide the authentication and cloud-sync services you have requested.

7.3 Legitimate Interests (Section 12(f), RA 10173)

Processing anonymized, aggregated performance and usage metrics is conducted in furtherance of our legitimate academic research interests, provided such processing does not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.

7.4 Compliance with Legal Obligations (Section 12(c), RA 10173)

We may process or retain data to the extent required to comply with applicable Philippine law, court orders, or regulatory requirements.

Data minimization: All processing is conducted on a need-to-know basis, limited to the minimum data necessary to fulfill the stated purpose.

Data Security

We implement reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information, including:

  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between the App and Supabase is encrypted using TLS/HTTPS.
  • Encryption at Rest: Data stored on Supabase is encrypted at rest by Supabase's infrastructure.
  • Password Security: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. Supabase Auth uses bcrypt hashing.
  • Row-Level Security (RLS): Supabase database tables are protected by RLS policies ensuring users can only access their own data.
  • Role-Based Access Control: Admin-level access is restricted to authorized accounts verified via the is_admin() function.
  • 6-Digit OTP Authentication: Account verification and password reset use time-limited OTPs to prevent link-scanner vulnerabilities.
  • Offline-First Architecture: Because core functionality is offline, your plant photographs are never exposed to network-layer security risks during identification.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach, we will notify you and the NPC in accordance with RA 10173 and NPC Circular No. 16-03.

Your Rights Under the Philippine Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)

These rights apply to data processed in Cloud-Sync Mode (account holders). No personal data is processed in Offline Mode.

§ 16(a) Right to Be Informed

You have the right to know whether your personal data is being processed, the source and purposes of processing, and the identity of the Personal Information Controller.

How we fulfill this: This Privacy Policy is the primary fulfillment, accessible within the App under Settings → Privacy Policy.

§ 16(b) Right to Object

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal data, including processing for direct marketing, automated profiling, or any other purpose not clearly disclosed.

How to exercise: Contact us using the details in Section 14. Upon receipt of a valid objection, we will cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds.

§ 16(c) Right to Access

You have the right to reasonable access to the contents of your personal data, the sources from which it was obtained, the purposes for which it is being processed, and the identities of any third parties to whom it has been disclosed.

How to exercise: Submit a written request via Section 14. We will respond within 15 business days.

§ 16(d) Right to Rectification

You have the right to dispute inaccurate or incomplete personal data and request correction without unreasonable delay.

How to exercise: Update your email directly via Settings → Change Email. For other corrections, contact us via Section 14.

§ 16(e) Right to Erasure or Blocking

You have the right to request deletion or blocking of your personal data where the data is incomplete, outdated, false, no longer necessary, or where you withdraw consent.

How to exercise: Delete your account via Settings → Delete Account, which triggers automatic deletion from Supabase. For manual requests, contact us via Section 14.

§ 18 Right to Data Portability

You have the right to obtain a copy of your personal data in an electronic or structured format where technically feasible.

How to exercise: Contact us via Section 14 with a portability request. We will provide your data in JSON format within 30 business days.

§ 16(f) Right to Damages

You have the right to be compensated for any damages sustained due to inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, false, unlawfully obtained, or unauthorized use of your personal data, in accordance with applicable law.

Right to Lodge a Complaint
