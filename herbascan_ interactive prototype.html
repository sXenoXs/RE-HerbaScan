Lagundi Details
🏷️ Taxonomy
Kingdom: Plantae Family: Lamiaceae Genus: Vitex Species: V. negundo
🌱 Morphology
Shrub with palmate leaves, 3-5 leaflets, aromatic when crushed. Purple flowers arranged in terminal spikes.
🌍 Ecology & Habitat
Found in tropical lowlands and riverbanks. Thrives in sunny, well-drained moist soils. Common in Southeast Asian regions with high humidity.
🏥 Medicinal Uses
Traditional treatment for cough, asthma, fever, and respiratory ailments. Also used for headaches and body pain.
